## 0.0.1
### 🚀 Initial Release

-   Introduced **MuiTypography**, a React-MUI-inspired typography widget
    for Flutter\
-   Added all major MUI variants (`h1` to `h6`, `subtitle1`, `body1`,
    etc.)\
-   Added `gutterBottom`, `noWrap`, and `paragraph` features\
-   Supports custom colors and TextTheme fallback
